Dataset: Pizza Place Sales
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mysarahmadbhat/pizza-place-sales
Author: mysarahmadbhat
License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
Last Updated: 2022-10-01
Description: A year's worth of sales from a fictitious pizza place (2015)

Files in this dataset:
- order_details.csv (1.31 MB) - Transactional line items with quantity per pizza
- orders.csv - Order header information with date and time
- pizza_types.csv - Pizza metadata (name, category, ingredients)
- pizzas.csv - Pizza pricing by size (S, M, L, XL, XXL)

Schema Relationship:
orders.order_id → order_details.order_id
order_details.pizza_id → pizzas.pizza_id
pizzas.pizza_type_id → pizza_types.pizza_type_id

Total Records:
- orders: 21,350 rows
- order_details: 48,620 rows
- pizzas: 96 rows (4 sizes × 32 pizza types)
- pizza_types: 32 rows

Usage in this project:
This dataset is used for Python-based analysis including:
- Business KPI calculation
- Statistical analysis and outlier detection
- Market basket analysis for cross-sell opportunities
- Time series decomposition and forecasting
- Data visualization with Matplotlib and Seaborn
