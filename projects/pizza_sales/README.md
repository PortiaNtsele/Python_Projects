## 🍕 Featured Project: Pizza Sales Analytics

This project delivers a comprehensive analysis of pizza sales data, progressing from basic business metrics to advanced predictive analytics.

### Key Focus Areas
*   **Business KPIs:** Calculated total revenue, average order value, and sales performance metrics.
*   **Statistical Analysis:** Analyzed revenue distribution, detected outliers, and performed correlation analysis to identify sales drivers.
*   **Market Basket Analysis:** Identified frequently bought-together pizza combinations and calculated lift for cross-sell opportunities.
*   **Time Series Analysis:** Decomposed daily revenue into trend, seasonal, and residual components to understand weekly patterns.
*   **Revenue Forecasting:** Implemented Holt-Winters exponential smoothing to forecast 14-day revenue with confidence intervals.

### 💡 Key Insights

| Insight | Business Implication |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Price-Insensitive Demand** | Weak correlation between price and revenue suggests customers prioritize variety/quality over cost. |
| **Top Combinations Identified** | The Hawaiian Pizza + The Thai Chicken Pizza shows lift > 1, indicating strong cross-sell potential. |
| **Weekly Seasonality** | Friday shows the highest seasonal boost (+$373); Sunday shows the lowest dip (-$331). |
| **Revenue Distribution** | Right-skewed with outliers; 1% of orders contribute significantly to total revenue. |
| **14-Day Forecast** | Projected revenue of $26,471 with identifiable weekly patterns. |

### 🎯 Recommendations
1.  **Bundle Strategy:** Create combo deals for frequently paired pizzas identified in basket analysis.
2.  **Marketing Focus:** Concentrate promotions on peak hours and high-performing days (Friday).
3.  **Operational Planning:** Adjust staffing levels based on weekly seasonality pattern.
4.  **Outlier Monitoring:** Track high-value outlier orders for potential VIP customer opportunities or fraud detection.
